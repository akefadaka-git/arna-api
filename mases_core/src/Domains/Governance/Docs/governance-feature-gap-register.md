## Non-negotiable Governance ownership boundaries

1. Governance owns governance bodies, positions, terms, appointments, memberships, seats, occupancy, acting authority, delegations, meetings, elections, resolutions, and formal authority grants.
2. Organization may link to Governance-owned records through explicit contracts; it must not duplicate Governance models, migrations, workflows, or authority semantics.
3. Organization assignment classifications `acting` and `delegated` are operational placement classifications only. They never create, imply, or replace a Governance authority instrument.
4. Governance production acceptance requires its own source review, migrations, tenant isolation, authorization, workflow transition, audit, app integration, and exact-SHA verification.
5. This register records capability gaps only; it does not authorize Governance implementation, migrations, workflow runs, PR updates, merges, or deployment.
