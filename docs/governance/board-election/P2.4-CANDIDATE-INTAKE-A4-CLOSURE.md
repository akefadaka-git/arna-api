## 2. A4 independent review

The final branch was reviewed against the repository's existing tenant, Person, User, Shareholder, Core Files, Authorization, Audit and committee-security contracts.

Verified controls:
- Candidate records are scoped to tenant and election and keyed to the canonical Person.
- Candidate Person must be active and have an active tenant relationship or active individual-shareholder relationship. Candidate login accounts are not required.
- Nominator must be an active User with active tenant membership, an active canonical Person identity, and an active shareholder mandate.
- Individual shareholder authority resolves through the direct \`user_id\` link; legal-entity authority resolves through the designated \`representative_user_id\` link. The two identity paths are not interchangeable.
- Ambiguous multiple-shareholder representation requires an explicit shareholder selection.
- Self-nomination is derived server-side from the canonical Person and direct individual-shareholder relationship; the client cannot set the flag.
- Nomination creation requires verified election authority and an actually open nomination window.
- Idempotent retries return the original accepted record; amendments create a new revision and preserve the superseded record; withdrawals are audited.
- Candidate evidence references a published, private, non-deleted Core File in the same tenant. SHA-256 is computed server-side from stored bytes.
- Evidence submitter cannot review their own submission. Evidence review is recorded separately from candidate eligibility.
- Candidate reads and mutations require active tenant membership and the relevant least-privilege permission.
- Candidate screening requires the candidate-screen permission and existing committee decision authorization, including active committee membership and the existing self-candidate/conflict/recusal checks.
- Committee screening disposition is appended to the existing hash-chained committee security event log.
- Required central audit persistence is fail-closed: if the audit write returns null, the candidate-domain transaction rolls back.
- New candidate permissions are seeded without automatic role grants.
- Cross-tenant candidate references are rejected.

## 3. Frozen functional boundary

The following are implemented:
- Candidate intake and canonical identity resolution.
- Shareholder and designated legal-entity representative nomination paths.
- Nomination timestamp window, idempotency, amendment history and withdrawal audit.
- Private evidence attachment, digest integrity and independent review.
- Committee-authorized unresolved eligibility screening.

The following remain deliberately unresolved and are not encoded as eligibility rules:
- Operative candidate qualification and disqualification criteria.
- Age, residency, shareholding threshold, tenure, conflict, or other statutory/Articles criteria.
- Candidate consent and declaration requirements.
- Share-class voting entitlements, vote weights and any one-vote-per-share rule.
- Committee size, role catalogue, seat allocation and quorum.
- Authoritative appeal procedure and final appeal adjudication.
- Any final \`eligible\` or \`ineligible\` determination.

The only eligibility disposition implemented in this slice is \`deferred_unresolved\`. A reviewed evidence record is not itself a legal eligibility determination.
