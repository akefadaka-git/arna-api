## 4. Historical candidate proposal disposition

The historical `authenticatedShareholderId()` helper matches `user_id` or `representative_user_id` but does not filter inactive or soft-deleted shareholder records. ## 5. Frozen P2.4 implementation contracts

### 5.1 Nomination authority and candidate identity
1. Every operation is tenant- and election-scoped and requires a verified election authority plus an open nomination window.
2. The API verifies `now() >= nominations_open_at` and `now() <= nominations_close_at`; `status = nominating` alone is insufficient.
3. A nominator must resolve to exactly one active, non-deleted shareholder record in the active tenant, linked either by direct individual `user_id` or designated `representative_user_id`. Ambiguous identities fail closed; an explicit shareholder selector may disambiguate only after revalidation.
4. Persist the nominating shareholder principal separately from the authenticated actor User and the actor's canonical Person identity.
5. A candidate must resolve to one active canonical Person with active tenant access. An active individual shareholder may also satisfy tenant association through its canonical Person link. A candidate need not be a shareholder or have a login account.
6. Self-nomination is server-derived from the authenticated shareholder's own canonical Person identity; a client boolean must not be trusted to relabel a third-party nomination or disguise a self-nomination.
7. A candidate's non-shareholder classification does not confer shareholder, voting, membership or tenant-admin rights.

### 5.2 Candidate, nomination and evidence aggregates
1. Keep one candidate aggregate per (tenant, election, canonical Person); allow distinct shareholder nominators to submit separate nomination records.
2. Preserve each submission, actor, nominator, statement, timestamp, idempotency key and correction/withdrawal history. Do not overwrite prior submissions with `updateOrInsert`.
3. Candidate evidence is distinct from election-authority evidence. Store references to private tenant-scoped Core Files records, evidence category/reference, digest, submitter, review status, reviewer, reason and timestamps.
4. Evidence review is separate from legal eligibility. Evidence existence or review does not itself make a candidate eligible.
5. Evidence submitter and reviewer must be different actors. Candidate screening/eligibility decisions must be attributable and append-only.
6. Candidate decision records must retain the applied rule version/reference, evidence references, rationale, actor and timestamp. A current status projection may be updated only transactionally with the immutable decision event.

### 5.3 Eligibility and committee decision controls
1. Candidate states distinguish intake, evidence completeness, screening, eligibility outcome and unresolved/appealed states. `submitted` or `intake_validated` never means `eligible`.
2. Committee decisions call the existing `assertMayDecide` service for the candidate Person, plus the candidate-specific least-privilege permission. Self-candidate, active conflict and recusal checks remain fail-closed.
3. The system must not hard-code candidate age, residency, shareholding, service, disqualification, family, qualification, director cap, nomination threshold or other company-specific eligibility criteria without an operative authoritative rule.
4. The repository evidence inspected here does not contain the current signed operative Articles/Bylaws and a complete approved candidate-qualification rule set. Until supplied and approved, the system may record evidence and an explicit `deferred_unresolved` disposition, but must not mark a candidate legally eligible or ineligible by inference.
5. Candidate decisions are human-governed. No AI, automation or shareholder rating determines eligibility.

### 5.4 Audit, concurrency and data integrity
- Use transactions and election/candidate row locks for duplicate prevention, evidence review, nomination correction/withdrawal and eligibility decisions.
- Use unique constraints for tenant/election candidate identity, idempotency keys and evidence identity; use append-only event/decision history for corrections.
- Every domain mutation writes central audit and, for committee decisions, the existing hash-chained committee security event. Required audit failure rolls back the domain transaction.
- Cross-tenant election, candidate, nominator, Person or File references fail closed.
- Keep candidate evidence private; never expose storage paths or file metadata outside authorized evidence endpoints.
- Do not place sensitive evidence contents or personal details in generic logs.

## 7. Required acceptance matrix

| Area | Positive proof | Required negative/regression proof |
|---|---|---|
| Nominator | Active individual shareholder and active designated legal-entity representative | Inactive/deleted shareholder, wrong tenant, no match, ambiguous multiple matches, inactive User |
| Candidate | Active Person shareholder and active non-shareholder Person with tenant access | Deleted/inactive Person, wrong tenant, missing tenant access, duplicate/ambiguous identity |
| Self-nomination | Authenticated individual shareholder nominates own canonical Person | Client-supplied self flag mismatch, different Person, legal-entity representative treated as owner |
| Nomination window | Valid submission within exact open/close timestamps | Future opening, after close, wrong election status, missing/unverified authority |
| Evidence | Private published same-tenant File; independent reviewer | Public file, cross-tenant file, deleted/archived file, missing file, same-user review, audit failure |
| Eligibility | Evidence-backed deferred-unresolved decision; eligible/ineligible only with approved versioned criteria | Evidence alone cannot grant eligibility; missing/unknown rules cannot be inferred; self/conflict/recused reviewer denied |
| Integrity | Idempotent retry, append-only correction/withdrawal, audit/event chain | Duplicate replay, concurrent duplicate, mutation of historical submission/decision |
| CI | Fresh MySQL migrations/seeds and candidate tests on exact SHA | SQLite-only success, stale-SHA workflow, pending migrations, non-idempotent rerun |
