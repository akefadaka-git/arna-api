## 3. Request text

I request a copy or extract of the current shareholder register maintained at the company's head office under Article 274 of the Commercial Code Proclamation No. 1243/2021.

Please provide the register as current on the date the copy/extract is prepared, including the particulars required by Article 274(2), in particular:

1. Each registered shareholder's name and address;
2. The number and identifying/numeration particulars of the shares registered to each shareholder;
3. The amount paid up on those shares; and
4. The date on which each shareholder was entered in the register.

Please include the current entries and the register's recorded amendments or changes necessary to make the copy/extract current as of its preparation date. If the register is maintained in more than one volume or format, please identify the records and date range covered by the supplied extract.

Please advise the prescribed fee and accepted payment method before or at submission, issue a receipt for any fee paid, and provide the copy/extract within the statutory period applicable under Article 274(4). Please identify the custodian or responsible officer and the date on which the extract was generated.

I request that the supplied copy/extract be signed, stamped or otherwise authenticated by the company where that is its normal practice, and that any unavailable field or withheld portion be specifically identified with the reason.

M3-E01 may be reconsidered only after the received source is assessed for:

- Issuer/custodian identity and authority;
- Date of issue and evidence of currentness;
- Completeness of all register pages/volumes and attachments;
