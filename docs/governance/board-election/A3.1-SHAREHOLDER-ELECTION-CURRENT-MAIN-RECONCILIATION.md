## 5. Binding integration contract

1. Shareholder owns holder identity, share classes, current holdings projection, append-only share ledger and certificates.
2. Governance owns election aggregate, authority evidence, candidate workflow, election-specific register snapshot, reconciliation/freeze, ballot custody, counting, disputes and certification.
3. Governance reads Shareholder data through a dedicated read-only adapter/service boundary. No Governance write to Shareholder tables, no direct update to holding projections, no duplicate shareholder master or share ledger.
4. Register preparation must capture tenant, election, approved rule-version reference, source record/version, source capture timestamp, record-date timestamp/timezone, preparer and canonical snapshot digest.
5. Historical balances must be reconstructed from immutable ledger business time, with explicit treatment of backdated entries and reversals. Current projection alone is insufficient.
6. A snapshot must preserve source-to-row provenance and explicit exceptions. Unverified, inactive, duplicate, conflicting, incomplete or stale source records must not silently enter a verified electorate.
7. Reconciliation and freeze require independent actors; preparer ≠ reconciler ≠ approver. Frozen rows/snapshot are immutable through application and database controls.
8. Snapshot and register lifecycle must be tenant/election scoped, audited and concurrency-safe. No cross-tenant references.
9. The App renders API decisions and statuses; it must not calculate eligibility, voting weights, quorum, tally or certification.
10. Candidate eligibility and voter eligibility are separate determinations. Shareholder status is not sufficient proof of candidate eligibility.

## 6. Legal/evidence gates — remain open
