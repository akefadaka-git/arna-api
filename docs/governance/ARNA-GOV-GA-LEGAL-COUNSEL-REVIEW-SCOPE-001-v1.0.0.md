# ARNA General Assembly — Independent Ethiopian Corporate-Law Review Scope

**Record:** ARNA-GOV-GA-LEGAL-COUNSEL-REVIEW-SCOPE-001  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Purpose:** Request for independent legal review and written opinion  
**Status:** Prepared; not sent; no counsel engaged and no fee authorized

## 1. Mandate

Provide an independent Ethiopian-law opinion authenticating the company's registered legal form and operative constitutional instruments, and determining the legal rules governing its General Meetings of Shareholders and related corporate records.

The reviewer must be independent of the company, its shareholders, directors, founders and current advisers. The reviewer must disclose actual or potential conflicts before receiving confidential company records.

## 2. Required reviewer credentials

Before engagement, obtain:
- Full name of responsible Ethiopian advocate/counsel.
- Current professional license/registration reference and issuing authority.
- Law-firm legal name and current firm registration reference.
- Confirmation of corporate/commercial law experience under Commercial Code Proclamation No. 1243/2021.
- Written conflict-of-interest clearance.
- Engagement letter, scope, deliverables, estimated completion date and fixed fee or pro-bono terms.

No engagement, document disclosure, fee payment or paid work is authorized by this scope document.

## 3. Documents to review

1. Current commercial registration certificate and official registry extract.
2. Certified Memorandum of Association and all schedules.
3. Certified Articles of Association, if separately registered/operative, and all schedules.
4. All registered amendments, restatements, capital changes, name changes, and registry endorsements.
5. Original full-resolution 17-page government/public-use signed/stamped document, including readable cover, signatures, stamps and endorsement.
6. Original full-resolution 22-page internal-use document, plus proof of adoption, approval organ, date, effective date and circulation status.
7. Any separate internal governance policies, shareholder agreements, Board rules, and corporate secretarial procedures.
8. Current shareholder register and share ledger, including share classes, allotments, transfers, paid/unpaid amounts, joint holdings and representative authority.
9. Founding and current Board appointment/election records.
10. Relevant General Meeting and Board notices, attendance, proxies, minutes and resolutions.
11. Current and historical business licenses and official registry records relevant to legal form and status.

Do not treat a transcription, working draft, internal manual, or unsigned copy as an authenticated constitutional instrument.

## 4. Questions requiring written determination

### A. Legal identity and form
- What is the exact registered legal name, legal form, registration number, registration date and current status?
- Is the company a share company under Commercial Code Proclamation No. 1243/2021?
- Which official registry documents establish this, and are there subsequent changes or transitional issues?

### B. Operative constitutional instruments
- What are the authoritative current constitutional instruments?
- Are the 17-page public-use and 22-page internal-use documents separate instruments, versions, consolidations, drafts, or copies?
- Which document was filed/registered, approved by the competent organ, and brought into force?
- What is the legal hierarchy between the registered Memorandum, Articles (if any), bylaws, internal policies, shareholder agreements and resolutions?
- Do any provisions conflict with mandatory law or later amendments?

### C. General Meeting lifecycle
For ordinary, extraordinary and special meetings, determine exact legal requirements and source provisions for:
- convening authority and valid calling process;
- notice lead time, delivery method, required contents, agenda and proof of service;
- quorum at first and adjourned meetings;
- voting eligibility, voting weights, share-class rights, restrictions and conflicts;
- proxy form, authority, eligibility, deadlines and revocation;
- chairing, tellers, information rights and vote counting;
- ordinary, special and extraordinary resolution thresholds;
- resolution effect, recording, challenge and filing requirements;
- minutes contents, signature, approval, custody and copies.

For each rule, identify whether it is mandatory statutory law, a permissible constitutional variation, a company-specific rule, or an internal policy.

### D. Shareholder register and voting basis
- Which register is legally authoritative on a meeting record date?
- What evidence establishes the current shareholder list and number/class of shares?
- Are any share classes subject to different voting rights, caps, restrictions or special consent?
- Is voting one vote per share, one vote per shareholder, or another basis for each matter?
- Are secret ballots required or permitted for any category of decision?

### E. Board and General Meeting authority
- Confirm matters reserved to the General Meeting, Board and management under the operative instruments and mandatory law.
- Review the validity of historical Board/General Meeting appointments and resolutions relevant to current authority.
- Identify any delegation, conflict-of-interest or related-party approval requirements.

### F. Penalties and financial consequences
- Determine whether the company may impose meeting lateness/absence penalties, the competent approving organ, due-process requirements, enforceability, accounting treatment and whether the amounts are statutory or purely company policy.
- Do not treat the owner-reported ETB 50 lateness and ETB 300 absence rules as legally activated without a clear source and valid approval.

## 5. Required deliverables

1. Signed legal opinion on registered legal form and operative instrument chain.
2. Certified-source register identifying each document, issuing authority, registration/approval details, version and effective date.
3. Clause-by-clause statutory/constitutional reconciliation matrix for all GA lifecycle requirements.
4. Explicit numerical and procedural rules that may safely be configured in ARNA, with source citations and any conditions.
5. List of invalid, ambiguous, superseded or unenforceable provisions.
6. Remediation plan identifying required corporate resolutions, amendments, filings, registry actions and deadlines.
7. Short implementation sign-off identifying which software controls may be activated and which must remain blocked.

## 6. Independence and confidentiality

The reviewer must not rely solely on the company's transcription or management summary. The reviewer should inspect the original certified instruments and registry records. All company documents must be handled confidentially and used only for this mandate. No document should be sent to a prospective firm until the owner approves the recipient and the scope of disclosure.

## 7. Commercial terms

Request:
- a no-obligation conflict check;
- a written fixed-fee quotation, or a pro-bono/low-cost option;
- a phased quote separating document authentication from full GA statutory analysis;
- a clear list of any official registry fees or third-party costs.

No fee, deposit, retainer or paid service is authorized by this document.

## 8. Software control

Until the signed opinion and authenticated primary documents are reviewed:
- legal-basis status remains pending;
- all statutory action gates remain blocked;
- no statutory quorum, notice period, voting weight, proxy deadline or resolution threshold is hardcoded;
- no production migration or deployment is authorized.
