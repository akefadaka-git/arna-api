### Important interpretation boundary

The bylaws' shareholder right to stand as a board candidate and the founding-document clause apparently requiring a director to hold at least one registered share during the term must be read together. The supplied text does not conclusively answer whether a non-shareholder may be nominated and then acquire a share before election/appointment, or whether shareholding is required at nomination. It also does not establish other possible statutory or company-specific qualifications. No system rule should be derived from that ambiguity.

The 145-share capital schedule and shareholder list do not, by themselves, prove a general one-share/one-vote rule, voting caps, share-class rights, or the current voter register.
