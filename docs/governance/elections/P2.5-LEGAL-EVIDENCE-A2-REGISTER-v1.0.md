## 3. Candidate eligibility — required legal decision matrix

Before any rule is encoded, the legal memorandum must identify, for each proposed criterion:

1. Exact statutory provision and current text.
2. Exact operative Memorandum/Articles clause and amendment reference.
3. Whether the criterion is mandatory statutory law, a valid company-specific rule, or not legally enforceable.
4. Whether it applies to every director or only a defined class/seat.
5. Whether it applies at nomination, election, appointment, and throughout the term.
6. Evidence required, issuing authority, verification method and permissible correction process.
7. Who may assess it, who decides, who reviews an objection/appeal, and how conflicts are handled.
8. Effective date and transition treatment for existing candidates/directors.
9. Any statutory exception or permitted non-shareholder director route.
10. Counsel's reasoned conclusion and the competent organ's recorded approval where required.

Until that matrix is complete, no age, residency, nationality, shareholding threshold, tenure, paid-up amount, disqualification, conflict, or other qualification is to be inferred or implemented.

The AGE A1 baseline requires candidate eligibility for shareholders and qualified non-shareholders. P2.4 technical intake supports a canonical Person identity without making intake equivalent to qualification. The only safe unresolved eligibility disposition remains \`deferred_unresolved\`.

## 4. Evidence acceptance and provenance protocol

For each external document:
- Preserve the original signed/certified document; do not overwrite or normalize the source.
- Record document title, issuer, instrument type, document date, effective date, registration/notarization details where applicable, amendment lineage, page count and custodian.
- Record who supplied it, when received, and the chain of custody.
- Store a private immutable source copy and calculate SHA-256 from the stored bytes.
- Record a separate verified transcription with page/paragraph/clause references; preserve the source language and any translation as distinct linked artifacts.
- Have counsel identify discrepancies, superseded provisions, conflicts and the exact current operative text.
- Require independent review of transcription and hash; a hash proves byte identity, not legal authenticity or legal effect.
- Record the approving authority and resolution reference separately from technical verification.
- Keep the evidence status pending/unverified until the required human and legal review is complete.

No public URL, OCR result or software-generated digest alone proves that a document is authentic, current, operative or legally controlling.

## 6. Fail-closed behavior required until evidence closure

- Candidate intake may record a nomination, but must not declare legal eligibility.
- No final \`eligible\` or \`ineligible\` state may be set using unapproved criteria.
- Objection/appeal records may be preserved as evidence, but no standing, filing deadline, admissibility, quorum, adjudicator, remedy, or finality may be inferred.
- Do not freeze a voter register or calculate final voting weight without verified source records and approved share-class rules.
- Do not activate electronic or hybrid voting until separate legal, Owner, competent-organ, security and A4 gates pass.
- No tally, automated result, or technical verification can certify an election.
- Conflicting, missing, stale or unauthenticated evidence must remain explicitly unresolved and block the affected transition.
