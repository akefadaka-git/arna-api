## 3. Statutory research — Commercial Code Proclamation No. 1243/2021

The English text of the Commercial Code reviewed for this engineering issue register contains the following provisions:

### Article 296 — Board composition

Article 296(1) provides for at least three and no more than thirteen directors elected by shareholders. Article 296(2) expressly permits persons who are not shareholders to be elected as directors, but limits non-shareholder directors to no more than one-third of the total board. Article 296(4) separately permits a legal-person entity to be appointed as director, with a permanent representative.

### Article 297 — Director qualifications

Article 297(1) lists qualification requirements, including any minimum age specified by the Memorandum or law, good moral character, specified criminal-conviction restrictions, and compliance with additional requirements set by the Memorandum or another law. Article 297(2) contains additional disqualification grounds and Article 297(3) provides a two-year bar for the specified fault.

### Article 298 — Appointment and term

Article 298(2) provides that subsequent directors are appointed by a general meeting. Article 298(3) sets a three-year term, subject to a contrary stipulation in the Memorandum, and permits re-election unless otherwise provided. Article 298(4) requires separate votes for each director where more than one director is elected.

### Article 300 — Chairperson

Article 300(1) specifically requires the chairperson to be a director who is a shareholder. This is distinct from the general rule permitting a limited number of non-shareholder directors under Article 296(2).

### Article 383–384 — Voting rights and vote limits

Article 383(1) provides that the weight of ordinary/dividend-share votes is proportional to the amount of capital represented. Article 383(2) provides that, absent legal deprivation, each share carries at least one vote. Article 384 allows the Memorandum to limit the highest number of votes exercisable at meetings, with the limit equal for all shares without distinction of class.

### Reconciliation issue — one-share director clause

The Code's general rule expressly permits non-shareholder directors up to one-third of the board (Article 296(2)). However, Article 297(1)(d) recognizes additional qualifications established by the Memorandum or another law. Article 300(1) separately requires a shareholder chairperson.

An earlier preliminary visual review reported a possible one-share condition, but the subsequent full page-by-page recovery does not reliably establish the exact Amharic wording or location. It is therefore not yet established that the bylaws contain this condition. If the clause is verified, it will still be necessary to determine:
1. whether it is part of, incorporated into, or separately adopted as the registered Memorandum term contemplated by Article 297(1)(d);
2. whether it is a valid additional company-specific qualification or conflicts with mandatory law;
3. whether it applies at nomination, election, appointment, or continuously during the term;
4. whether it can coexist with the statutory non-shareholder-director allowance; or
5. whether it was superseded or modified by a later registered instrument or resolution.

**Engineering disposition:** preserve the statutory non-shareholder route as a legal possibility; do not automatically reject non-shareholder nominees, and do not automatically disregard the apparent company clause. Keep final eligibility `deferred_unresolved` until the registered Memorandum, the complete operative corporate instrument set, and a written Ethiopian-law opinion resolve the interaction.
