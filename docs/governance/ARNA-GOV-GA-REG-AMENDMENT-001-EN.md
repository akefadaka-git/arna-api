# Maale Arna Share Company
## Draft Amendment to the General Assembly Procedure Regulation No. 001/2016

**Document ID:** ARNA-GOV-GA-REG-AMENDMENT-001-EN  
**Status:** Proposed draft for review — not adopted  
**Draft date:** 5 November 2026 (26 Tikimt 2019 E.C.)  
**Instrument amended:** General Assembly Procedure Regulation No. 001/2016  
**Company:** Maale Arna Share Company

> **Important notice:** This document is a working draft for review. It is not legal advice, a Board resolution, or a General Assembly resolution. It must be reconciled against the Company's certified Memorandum and Articles of Association, current shareholder register, prior valid resolutions, and applicable Ethiopian law. Independent Ethiopian corporate-law counsel must review the final text before it is submitted for approval. Statutory references and their application must be verified against the authoritative text.

## Preamble

This proposed amendment is prepared to support lawful, orderly, transparent, fair, and accountable General Assembly proceedings; protect shareholders' rights to participate, speak, obtain information, and vote; and ensure that notices, attendance, proxies, deliberations, voting outcomes, resolutions, and minutes are properly evidenced and retained.

This draft is intended to be reconciled with the Ethiopian Commercial Code Proclamation No. 1243/2021 and the Company's operative constitutional documents. It does not override any mandatory law, the Company's Memorandum and Articles of Association, or a valid corporate resolution.

## Article 1 — Short Title, Application, and Scope

1.1 This instrument may be cited as the “Amendment to the Maale Arna Share Company General Assembly Procedure Regulation No. 001/2016.”

1.2 Once duly approved and effective, this amendment shall be read together with the original Regulation. Only provisions expressly amended or repealed by this instrument are replaced. Unamended provisions remain in force to the extent that they are consistent with applicable law and the Company's constitutional documents.

1.3 This amendment does not replace the Commercial Code, the Company's Memorandum and Articles of Association, or any superior legal instrument. In the event of inconsistency, the applicable legal hierarchy governs.

## Article 2 — Definitions

For purposes of this amendment:

2.1 **Company** means Maale Arna Share Company.

2.2 **General Assembly** means an ordinary or extraordinary/special shareholders' meeting convened under applicable law and the Company's constitutional documents.

2.3 **Shareholder** means a person whose shareholder status and relevant rights are established under applicable law and the Company's authoritative records.

2.4 **Proxy** means a person authorized to represent a shareholder at a General Assembly in accordance with applicable law and the permitted proxy procedure.

2.5 **Record date / entitlement date** means the date, if validly established under applicable law and the Company's constitutional documents, used to determine entitlement to attend, be represented, and vote at a particular meeting. No record date shall be presumed or created by system configuration alone.

2.6 **Meeting record** means the notice and service evidence, attendance register, proxy instruments, agenda papers, voting records, resolutions, minutes, and associated supporting evidence.

## Article 3 — Authority to Convene a General Assembly

3.1 A General Assembly may be convened only by a person or body authorized to do so under the Commercial Code and the Company's operative constitutional documents.

3.2 The convening instrument shall identify the convening authority and its legal basis, the type of meeting, date, time, venue, and agenda.

3.3 A person without lawful convening authority shall not independently call, cancel, or alter a General Assembly.

## Article 4 — Notice, Service, and Evidence

4.1 The method, timing, and evidence of service of a General Assembly notice shall comply with the Commercial Code and the Company's constitutional documents.

4.2 The notice shall contain the Company's name, type of meeting, date, time, venue, detailed agenda, and any other information required by law.

4.3 The first ordinary or special meeting notice period described in Article 370 of the Commercial Code, including the 24-day period, and the distinct rules governing subsequent meetings must be verified by counsel and applied as required by law. This draft does not shorten a statutory notice period.

4.4 The Company shall retain evidence showing to whom notice was sent, when it was sent, the method used, and the delivery or transmission result. A digital transmission record does not, by itself, establish legally sufficient service unless the applicable legal requirements are met.

## Article 5 — Agenda and Additional Business

5.1 The General Assembly shall conduct business on the agenda stated in the valid notice, subject to exceptions expressly permitted by law or the Company's constitutional documents.

5.2 Shareholders' rights to request additional agenda items, including any ownership threshold, deadline, and notice procedure, shall be administered in accordance with Article 372 of the Commercial Code and the Company's operative constitutional documents.

5.3 A matter outside the notified agenda shall not be submitted for decision unless a lawful exception applies.

## Article 6 — Shareholder Register and Voting Entitlement

6.1 Entitlement to attend, appoint a proxy, and vote shall be determined by the authoritative shareholder register, the class and rights of the relevant shares, and applicable law.

6.2 The meeting register shall accurately show, to the extent required by law, the shareholders entitled to participate, their shareholdings and classes, voting rights, and any lawful restrictions.

6.3 Unless expressly established by law or the Company's constitutional documents, the Company shall not assume either “one shareholder, one vote” or “one equal vote per share.”

6.4 Any restriction on participation or voting must have a clear legal basis and be recorded with its supporting evidence.

## Article 7 — Attendance and Attendance Register

7.1 Participants shall arrive sufficiently before the meeting to verify identity and entitlement. The original Regulation's requirement to arrive 30 minutes before the meeting remains applicable unless validly amended.

7.2 The attendance register shall record the shareholder's name and address, whether attendance is personal or by proxy, the relevant shareholding, and voting entitlement as required by law.

7.3 The attendance record shall be verified by the responsible officer before the meeting. Every correction shall preserve who made it, when, why, and the supporting evidence.

## Article 8 — Proxies

8.1 A shareholder may be represented by a proxy in accordance with applicable law and the Company's constitutional documents.

8.2 The proxy instrument shall identify the shareholder and proxy, the authority granted, the meeting to which it applies, and any other particulars required by law.

8.3 The original Regulation requires a General Assembly proxy to be submitted to the head office at least three days before the meeting. This deadline must be reconciled by independent counsel with Article 377 of the Commercial Code and the Company's constitutional documents. Pending that review, this draft neither silently repeals nor conclusively validates the original deadline.

8.4 Where applicable law provides that a shareholder represented by proxy may not also personally participate or vote at the same meeting, that statutory rule shall be applied.

8.5 Notarization or authentication of a proxy shall not be imposed as an additional mandatory condition unless required by applicable law.

## Article 9 — Quorum, Voting Thresholds, and Validity of Resolutions

9.1 Quorum and voting thresholds shall be determined by the applicable provisions of the Commercial Code and the Company's constitutional documents.

9.2 Ordinary and extraordinary/special General Assemblies may be subject to different quorum and voting rules. A rule applicable to one type of meeting shall not be imported into the other.

9.3 The ordinary-meeting rules described in Article 398 of the Commercial Code, including the first and subsequent meeting requirements, and the extraordinary-meeting rules described in Article 402 must be checked against the authoritative statutory text and the Company's circumstances by independent counsel before final adoption.

9.4 Quorum shall be calculated only from verified voting shares represented in person or through legally valid proxies. The meeting system shall not declare quorum where the underlying register, proxy evidence, or share-voting data is incomplete or unverified.

9.5 The quorum calculation, shares represented, participants, votes cast, abstentions, and result of each resolution shall be recorded in the minutes and supporting evidence.

## Article 10 — Chairperson, Secretary, and Scrutineers

10.1 The chairperson shall be determined in accordance with the Commercial Code and the Company's constitutional documents. The prescribed order of succession or election shall be followed.

10.2 The chairperson shall administer proceedings, maintain an orderly speaking sequence, and ensure that decisions are evidenced. This authority shall not be used to remove a shareholder right granted by law.

10.3 The secretary shall coordinate the attendance register, agenda proceedings, resolution texts, voting records, and minutes.

10.4 Scrutineers or vote counters shall be appointed or selected in the manner permitted by law or duly approved meeting procedure and shall retain the counting evidence.

## Article 11 — Deliberations, Questions, Motions, and Voting

11.1 Shareholders may ask questions, seek explanations, and make relevant statements on agenda matters, subject to applicable law and orderly procedure.

11.2 A motion submitted for decision shall be clearly worded, linked to the relevant agenda item, and identify its proposer or sponsoring body where required.

11.3 Voting method, voting entitlement and weight, required majority, and treatment of abstentions or blank ballots shall be governed by applicable law and the Company's constitutional documents.

11.4 Electronic voting or remote participation is not authorized by this draft. It may be introduced only where expressly permitted by applicable law and the Company's constitutional documents and governed by a separately approved procedure that addresses identity, eligibility, secrecy where applicable, integrity, auditability, and evidence.

## Article 12 — Conduct and Procedural Measures

12.1 Participants shall attend on time, seek recognition before speaking, address relevant agenda matters, respect the rights of others, and refrain from disrupting proceedings.

12.2 Prohibited conduct includes disruption, insults or intimidation, presenting unauthorized business for decision, disrupting the speaking order, leaving without permission where permission is required by the original Regulation, and other conduct listed in the original Regulation.

12.3 Any procedural measure shall be proportionate to the conduct and may include an oral warning, written warning, temporary interruption of a speech, or removal from the meeting in serious circumstances. No measure may suspend a statutory participation or voting right without a lawful basis and due process.

12.4 A chairperson shall not, acting alone and at discretion, suspend voting rights, suspend membership, or impose a penalty extending beyond the meeting. Any such measure requires a lawful source of authority and the applicable procedure.

## Article 13 — Monetary Penalties, Evidence, Review, and Appeal

13.1 The original Regulation states penalties of ETB 50 for late arrival and ETB 300 for absence without sufficient justification. This draft does not increase, decrease, or create any monetary penalty.

13.2 Until legality, authority, enforceability, and proportionality are confirmed by independent counsel, an assessed penalty shall not automatically be treated as a finally established debt.

13.3 A penalty case shall document the alleged event, date and time, evidence source, recorded conduct, and any explanation provided by the affected person.

13.4 The person who records or assesses a penalty shall not be the sole final approver of that penalty. An independent reviewer shall examine the evidence. The affected person shall have a reasonable opportunity to respond and to appeal under a defined procedure.

13.5 A penalty shall not be automatically deducted from dividends or another shareholder payment. Any set-off or collection requires a lawful basis, an authorized decision, and a separate compliant Finance process.

## Article 14 — Adjournment, Suspension, and Continuation

14.1 Adjournment, suspension, or continuation of a General Assembly shall be decided only by the person or body authorized under the Commercial Code and the Company's constitutional documents.

14.2 The reason, decision, continuation date and time, and notice given to participants shall be recorded in the minutes.

14.3 Notice, quorum, and agenda rules for second or subsequent meetings shall follow the distinct statutory provisions applicable to those meetings.

## Article 15 — Resolutions and Execution

15.1 A General Assembly resolution shall be clear, specific, related to the agenda, and supported by a valid voting result.

15.2 Where applicable, the resolution shall identify the responsible body, execution deadline, required resources, and evidence by which completion will be verified.

15.3 Resolutions shall be executed by the body authorized under law and the Company's governance structure. A software system may record and track a resolution but shall not replace the legal decision-maker or confer authority.

## Article 16 — Minutes, Records, and Evidence Retention

16.1 Minutes shall contain the particulars required by law, participant information, material proceedings, resolution texts, voting results, objections, and assigned follow-up actions.

16.2 Minutes shall be signed, certified, and retained by the person or body prescribed by law and the Company's constitutional documents.

16.3 Meeting records shall be retained in a controlled repository with appropriate access permissions, change history, and evidence traceability. Retention periods shall follow applicable law and the Company's records policy.

## Article 17 — Calendar and Digital Administration

17.1 Meeting date, time, venue, status, and authorized changes may be displayed in the Company's approved calendar system.

17.2 The authoritative meeting record remains the Governance meeting record. The calendar is a projection for display, discovery, and reminders. If the two differ, the authorized source record governs.

17.3 The Ethiopian calendar shall be the default display. Gregorian dates may be shown through an authorized user preference or where needed for legal clarity. Any date conversion relied upon as evidence shall be recorded.

17.4 A calendar entry or reminder is not a statutory meeting notice and does not replace legally sufficient service.

## Article 18 — Transitional Provision and Effective Date

18.1 This amendment shall become effective only after completion of the required approval process and any legal formalities.

18.2 Meeting processes commenced before the effective date shall be handled under the applicable law and valid rules governing those processes. This amendment shall not retrospectively validate or invalidate prior resolutions.

18.3 Any filing, registration, or notice required by law shall be completed before the amendment is treated as effective.

## Article 19 — Approval and Further Amendment

19.1 Article 9 of the original Regulation states that the Board shall decide amendments unanimously and that an amendment is accepted only after General Assembly approval. The validity and interaction of that procedure with the Commercial Code and the Company's constitutional documents must be confirmed by counsel.

19.2 The preamble to the original Regulation refers to Article 28.34 of the Company's Memorandum as the source for preparing internal Board and General Assembly rules for approval. The exact text and scope of the certified constitutional provision must be checked before final adoption.

19.3 This draft does not approve itself. Each approval and legal formality must be evidenced in the approval register below.

## Schedule A — Original Provisions Intended to Continue

Unless expressly amended by an approved instrument, the following original provisions are intended to continue:

- **Original Article 5:** punctual attendance; advance submission of proxy documents; arrival 30 minutes before the meeting; seeking recognition before speaking; speaking to agenda matters; not leaving without permission; and observing meeting procedure.
- **Original Article 6:** prohibited conduct, including disruption, unauthorized speaking, leaving without permission, and other procedural violations.
- **Original Article 7:** categories of warnings and procedural measures. These shall be applied subject to the legality, proportionality, independent review, and appeal safeguards in Articles 12 and 13 of this draft.
- **Penalty amounts:** ETB 50 for late arrival and ETB 300 for absence without sufficient justification. These amounts are not changed by this draft; their legal basis and enforceability remain subject to counsel's review.
