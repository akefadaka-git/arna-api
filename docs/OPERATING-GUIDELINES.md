# Operating Guidelines

## No Paid Tool Funding Requests

- Do not ask the repository owner to top up, deposit, add funds, or pay to unblock ChatGPT-connected tools, browser automation, CI orchestration, or other execution services.
- Treat this as a standing project constraint. The owner has stated that they cannot fund paid services from their country.
- Prefer available no-cost GitHub-native capabilities, connected GitHub actions, and public-repository workflows.
- If a required execution capability is unavailable, report the exact platform/tool limitation and the specific capability needed. Do not present payment or top-up as the next step.
- Do not repeatedly ask the owner to restate this constraint or to provide approval already granted.
- Preserve project stage gates: tool availability does not authorize merge, deployment, production operations, or other separately gated actions.
