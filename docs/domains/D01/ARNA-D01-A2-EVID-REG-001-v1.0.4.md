## 1. Owner clarification — document purpose

The Owner clarified on 2026-11-25 that:
- the **17-page signed/stamped bylaws scan** is intended for government and public use;
- the **22-page bylaws/founding-text document** is intended for internal company use.

These are two purpose-specific company instruments/evidence sources. They must not be collapsed into a single “old vs. new version” narrative. Purpose classification is an Owner-provided fact; it does not, by itself, prove registration, adoption, amendment, legal priority, effective date or present legal force.

The 17-page instrument remains the public/government-facing source for the purpose stated by the Owner. The 22-page document remains the internal-use source for the purpose stated by the Owner. Neither is automatically assumed to override the other. Any internal clause that purports to amend, contradict or enlarge the public/registered instrument's external legal effect requires competent-organ and Ethiopian corporate-law review.

## 4. Data integrity and legal controls

1. The 29-entry/145-share schedule and the 54-entry/560-share schedule are historical document schedules, not proof of current ownership, current paid-up capital, valid calls, voting entitlements, transfers or the present electorate.
2. Do not use either schedule as the live shareholder register or seed ARNA production data.
3. Do not infer current directors from the five- or seven-person founding lists.
4. Do not apply the internal re-election restriction, one-share director requirement or any quorum interpretation to a live election until the operative certified instrument and mandatory law have been checked.
5. Do not assume that internal-use purpose means the 22-page document is unadopted. Equally, do not assume it is approved or legally effective solely because it is used internally.
6. Do not assume that public/government-facing purpose proves the 17-page scan was registered or remains the latest legally operative instrument.
7. Preserve both sources independently, including original bytes, provenance, page count, file hash, custodian, receipt date and any certification/registration metadata once obtained.

## 5. Evidence still required to resolve legal instrument hierarchy

The following are unresolved and cannot be manufactured from the two texts:

- certified/registered public-facing instrument, including legible registration number, issuing authority, date and authenticated copy;
- original or certified internal-use instrument and evidence of its approval/adoption, effective date, approving organ and amendment history;
- Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association, including all registered amendments;
- General Meeting and Board resolutions that adopted, amended, superseded or authorized internal bylaws;
- documentary explanation for the 29/145-share versus 54/560-share schedules and reconciliation to the current shareholder register and capital ledger;
- documentary basis for five versus seven founding directors and current director appointments/terms;
- competent-organ legal determination of any conflicting clauses, including quorum, re-election and director shareholding;
- named current corporate custodian/secretary and verified authority to certify the instruments.

## 6. D01 impact and decision

D01 needs an authoritative strategy approval authority and valid decision record. The two bylaws sources clarify governance context but do not establish:
- which current organ/office-holder is competent to approve the enterprise strategy;
- the current Board/executive/planning-process appointments and delegation chain;
- an adopted enterprise strategy resolution and effective strategy baseline;
- a verified legal hierarchy among the Memorandum, Articles, public-facing bylaws and internal-use bylaws.

D03 governance records and D20 document custody may eventually provide structured evidence, but a database record or uploaded file cannot replace certified legal evidence or a competent legal determination.
